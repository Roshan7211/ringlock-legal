Ringlock support

Ringlock is a puzzle of metal rings laid on a jeweller's tray. Where two rings cross, one lies on top and holds the other. Turn a ring until its own gap sits under the ring holding it, lift it out, and free the charm in the middle. Every charm you free goes on your bracelet. There is a new Daily Lock every day, a long trail of levels, and a Lock Bench where you cut locks of your own and share them by a six-letter code. Your progress stays on your device, with no account. The game shows ads from Google: now and then between locks, and a short video for a hint once the day's three free hints are used.

How to play

  • Drag a ring round to turn it, one click at a time.
  • Two hooked rings cross twice, once each way up, so each holds the other at one place: where it lies on top.
  • A ring comes out only through its own gap. Turn its gap under every ring lying over it, then tap it to lift it out.
  • Tap a ring that is still held and a red mark shows where.
  • Frozen rings cannot turn until a ring hooked through them comes out.

These rules are always in Settings → How to play.

Questions

How are moves counted? Every click a ring turns is one move, counted the short way round. Pulling a ring out is free, and so is Undo.

How do I get three stars? Open the lock in par moves or fewer without a hint. A little over par earns two stars. A hint takes one ring out for you, and caps the lock at two stars. You get three free hints a day; after that a short video earns each hint.

How do lock codes work? Every lock has a six-letter code. At the Lock Bench, type a friend's code under A friend's lock and your phone makes the very same lock — nothing is sent anywhere. A code from an older version of Ringlock may not open; the app says so.

When does the Daily Lock change? At midnight in your phone's own time zone. Everyone gets the same lock that day, and you can replay it as often as you like — your best result is kept.

Can I turn off sounds or vibration? Yes, in Settings. On iPhone, sounds also stay quiet when the silent switch is on.

I left a lock half done. It is kept: the home screen shows Resume.

Your data

Your levels, charms, stars, streaks, Lock Bench locks and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. The ads are Google's; you can change your ad choices in Settings where the law asks for it, and report an ad there too. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
