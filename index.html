Ringlock support

Ringlock is a wooden ring puzzle: turn the metal rings until their gaps line up, pull them out one by one, and free the charm in the middle. Every charm you free goes on your bracelet. There is a new Daily Lock every day and a long trail of levels. It works entirely on your device — no account, no ads, no tracking.

Contact

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? E-mail infotech@hexavo.co.uk and include your phone model and iOS version.

How to play

  • Drag a ring round to turn it, one click at a time.
  • Tap a ring to pull it out. It comes out only when nothing is hooked through it.
  • Two rings are hooked where they cross. They come unhooked when either ring's gap sits over that crossing.
  • The iron ring in the middle has no gap, so a ring hooked on it must use its own.
  • Tug a ring that is still hooked and the crossing holding it flashes red.
  • Frozen rings cannot turn until a ring hooked through them comes out.

These rules are always in Settings.

Questions

How are moves counted? Every click a ring turns is one move, counted the short way round. Pulling a ring out is free, and so is Undo.

How do I get three stars? Open the lock in par moves or fewer without a hint. A little over par earns two stars; a hint caps the lock at two stars.

When does the Daily Lock change? At midnight in your phone's own time zone. Everyone gets the same lock that day, and you can replay it as often as you like — your best result is kept.

Can I turn off sounds or vibration? Yes, in Settings. Sounds also stay quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on.

I left a lock half done. It is kept: the home screen shows Resume.

Your data

Your levels, charms, stars, streaks and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
