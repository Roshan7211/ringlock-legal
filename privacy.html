Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

Ringlock is made by Hexavo, which is responsible for the app and for this policy. Questions or requests about your privacy: infotech@hexavo.co.uk.

What Ringlock collects

Ringlock itself does not collect, send or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and nothing you do in the game leaves your device. Hexavo receives no information about you from the app.

Ads

Ringlock shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between locks, and a short video you can choose to watch for a hint once the day's free hints are used. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's IP address (which can show your approximate location), your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad, only if you allow tracking when asked), which ads you see and tap, and app performance and crash data. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, under Ad choices. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking, or on Android reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads (on some phones, Privacy, Ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for everyone. How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ringlock itself never sees or keeps it.

What stays on your phone

Your levels, charms, stars, streaks, name, settings, the locks you have opened at the Lock Bench and any lock in progress are saved on your device only, so you can pick up where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them. If your phone backs up app data, they are included in that backup, which is yours.

Sharing

When you share a daily result, the app makes a picture and a line of text and hands them to your phone's share sheet. When you challenge a friend, it hands over a line of text with a lock's six-letter code and your result. You choose where they go; the app sends nothing itself. A lock's code describes only the lock, never you. If you choose Save Image, your phone asks your permission before the picture is added to your photos; the app never reads your photos.

Copying a code

The Lock Bench can copy a lock's code to your clipboard when you tap the copy button. The app does not read your clipboard; you type or paste a friend's code yourself.

Ratings

Now and then the app asks the App Store or Google Play to show its own rating box. Any rating or review you give goes to the store, under your store account; the app never sees it.

Security

Ringlock sends nothing to Hexavo, so there is no copy of your information on our side to protect or lose. Google's ad software talks to Google only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and Google protects what it collects under its own policy.

Your choices and deleting your data

Ringlock keeps nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to hold or delete: your game data exists only on your device, and deleting the app removes it. To withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time, use Ad choices in the game's Settings (where shown), or switch off tracking for Ringlock in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking, or on Android delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Google, Ads. Information Google collects to show ads is kept and deleted under Google's own policy; you can review and manage it at https://myadcenter.google.com and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, to object to its use and to withdraw consent; write to infotech@hexavo.co.uk and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Children

Ringlock is not directed at children, and Ringlock itself collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
